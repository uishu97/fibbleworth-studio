Fibbleworth Studio

The workshop behind Captain Fibbleworth's tall tales.

Captain Fibbleworth is an animated series of sea stories on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, told by an old captain who swears every word is true. Fibbleworth Studio is the private tool the series' creator uses to make each episode and post it to the Captain Fibbleworth channel and the series' Instagram account and Facebook Page.

What it does

  • Turns a written story into a narrated, animated short video, on the creator's own computer.
  • Sends each finished video to the creator for review. Nothing is posted until the creator approves it.
  • Uploads approved videos to the Captain Fibbleworth YouTube channel as private videos, with their title, description and tags.
  • Posts approved videos as Reels on the Captain Fibbleworth Instagram account and Facebook Page, with their caption.

Who uses it

Only the channel's creator. Fibbleworth Studio is not offered to anyone else: there is no sign-up, and it never uses another person's Google, YouTube, Facebook or Instagram account.

Connected accounts

Fibbleworth Studio signs in with Google so it can upload videos to the Captain Fibbleworth channel through the YouTube Data API, and with Facebook so it can post Reels to the Captain Fibbleworth Page and Instagram account through Meta's Graph API. The privacy policy explains what it can access, what it keeps and how to remove its access.
