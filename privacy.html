Privacy policy

Fibbleworth Studio · last updated 26 October 2026

Fibbleworth Studio is a private tool used only by the creator of Captain Fibbleworth to post the series' own videos to its YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page. It has no other users and collects nothing from viewers.

Who is responsible

The creator of Captain Fibbleworth (youtube.com/@fibbleworth) runs Fibbleworth Studio and this website. Contact: hello@fibbleworth.com.

YouTube API Services

Fibbleworth Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Fibbleworth Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Meta Platform

Fibbleworth Studio posts to the Captain Fibbleworth Facebook Page and Instagram account through Meta's Graph API, under the Meta Platform Terms. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

What Fibbleworth Studio accesses

When the creator connects the channel, Google asks them to allow Fibbleworth Studio to upload and manage the channel's YouTube videos. Fibbleworth Studio uses that permission for one thing: uploading a video the creator has approved, with its title, description and tags, and reading back YouTube's reply about that upload (the new video's ID and its upload and privacy status). It does not read email, contacts, files, viewing history, comments or any other Google data.

When the creator connects the Facebook Page, Meta asks them to allow Fibbleworth Studio to see the Page and the Instagram account linked to it and to publish to them. Fibbleworth Studio uses that permission for one thing: posting a video the creator has approved as a Reel with its caption, and reading back Meta's reply about it (the post's ID, its processing status and its link). It does not read messages, comments, followers, insights or any other Facebook or Instagram data.

What it keeps, and where

  • The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue, stored encrypted in the n8n automation software on the creator's own computer. They are used only to post videos.
  • For each posted video: its YouTube, Instagram or Facebook ID, link and upload status, saved next to the episode on the creator's computer so the same video is never posted twice.
  • n8n's record of each run, including YouTube's and Meta's replies, on the same computer. n8n deletes these records automatically after 14 days.

Nothing is stored on servers run by Fibbleworth Studio; it has none. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Who it is shared with

Data from Google and Meta is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The video and its details go only to the platform it is posted on, as the post itself. After a post, its link is sent to the creator's own private chat with the studio's Telegram bot, so the creator can check it.

Fibbleworth Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing its access and deleting data

Google access can be withdrawn at any time on Google's Third-party apps and services page or the security settings page. Facebook and Instagram access can be withdrawn under Facebook's Business integrations settings by removing Fibbleworth Studio. After that the stored tokens no longer work, and they are deleted from the creator's computer. To ask for any data described here to be deleted, write to hello@fibbleworth.com; it is deleted within 30 days. Videos already posted stay on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook until they are deleted there.

Cookies

Fibbleworth Studio and this website set no cookies and use no local storage on your device.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The pages load nothing from any other site.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is held, and have it corrected or deleted, by writing to hello@fibbleworth.com. If you live in the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Fibbleworth Studio is used only by the channel's adult creator and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
