Privacy policy

Fibbleworth Studio · in effect from 26 October 2026

Fibbleworth Studio is a private tool used only by the creator of the Captain Fibbleworth YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos. It has no other users and collects nothing from viewers of the channel.

Who is responsible

The creator of Captain Fibbleworth (youtube.com/@fibbleworth) runs Fibbleworth Studio and this website. Contact: hello@fibbleworth.com.

YouTube API Services

Fibbleworth Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using Fibbleworth Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Fibbleworth Studio accesses

When the creator connects the channel, Google asks them to allow Fibbleworth Studio to upload and manage the channel's YouTube videos. Fibbleworth Studio uses that permission for one thing: uploading a video the creator has approved, with its title, description and tags, and reading back YouTube's reply about that upload (the new video's ID and its upload and privacy status). It does not read email, contacts, files, viewing history, comments or any other Google data.

What it keeps, and where

  • The sign-in tokens Google issues, stored encrypted in the n8n automation software on the creator's own computer. They are used only to make uploads.
  • For each uploaded video: its YouTube ID and upload status, saved next to the episode on the creator's computer so the same video is never uploaded twice.
  • n8n's record of each upload run, including YouTube's reply, on the same computer. n8n deletes these records automatically after 14 days.

Nothing is stored on servers run by Fibbleworth Studio; it has none. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Who it is shared with

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The video and its details go only to YouTube, as the upload itself. After an upload, the video's link is sent to the creator's own private chat with the studio's Telegram bot, so the creator can check it.

Fibbleworth Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing its access

Access can be withdrawn at any time on Google's Third-party apps and services page or the security settings page. After that the stored tokens no longer work, and they are deleted from the creator's computer. To ask for any data described here to be deleted, write to hello@fibbleworth.com; it is deleted within 30 days. Videos already uploaded stay on YouTube until they are deleted there.

Cookies

Fibbleworth Studio and this website set no cookies and use no local storage on your device.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The pages load nothing from any other site.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you is held, and have it corrected or deleted, by writing to hello@fibbleworth.com. If you live in the European Union, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Fibbleworth Studio is used only by the channel's adult creator and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
